
> A cooperative asymmetric game for 2 players.

---

## Overview

Two players work together to resolve a timeline without accumulating too many bad outcomes. One player is the **Anchor** — a person living through time linearly. The other is the **Weaver** — a time traveler who scouts ahead and tries to prepare the Anchor for what's coming.

---

## Components

- A deck of **Time Slot cards** (the timeline)
- A deck of **Information cards** (with types and credit values)
- **Bad tokens** (three types: Media, Tech, Force)
- **Good tokens** (three types: Resources, Resistance, Leverage)

---

## Setup

1. Place the starting card on the table.
2. Shuffle the time slot cards, place 12 in a row next to the right of the starting card. Return the rest to the box.
3. Shuffle the information cards and place 3 Information cards face down above each time slot.
4. Give each player 3 information cards. Return the rest of the information cards to the box.
5. The Anchor and Weaver begin at the starting card.
6. The Weaver takes their first turn.

---

## The Cards

### Information Cards
Each Resource card has:
- A **type** - Resources, Resistance, Leverage
- A **value** - Signifies cost to play by the Anchor or Movement by Weaver
- An **effect** - (triggered when the Anchor plays it)

### Time Slot Cards
Each Time Slot card will add a negative token (Media, Tech, Force) unless the anchor pays the cost specified.

---

## The Weaver

### Turn Structure
The Weaver will repeat the following steps until they choose to stop.
- Play a card from their hand to time travel.
- Look at the 3 cards in the current time slot, choose one to keep, one to discard and one to leave for the anchor.

## Time Travel
The weaver plays an information card from their hand and move to a time slot up to the value of the card away forward or backwards in time.
The weaver may never visit a time slot they already visited.
The weaver may not visit the time slot where the anchor is present.
The weaver may not visit a time slot further in the past than the anchor.

---

## The Anchor

### Turn Structure
The anchor repeats the following steps until they reach the weaver, then the weaver takes their turn.
1. Arrive at the current time slot
2. Flip the Time Slot card and resolve the effect.
3. Look at the information cards above the time slot, pick one to add to their hand and discard the rest.
4. Play information card from their hand paying the cost or discard an information card to gain credits.

### Playing cards
To play an information card the anchor must pay credits equal to the card's value, with a discount of 1 for each card of that type they have.


### Credits
If the anchor cannot or chooses not to play a card they must discard one information card to gain a number of credits equal to the value of the card.


---

## Co-Presence & Communication
- When both players are at the same slot simultaneously, they may **talk freely**
- When the players are at different time slot they may not communicate.

---

## Loss Conditions

The players **lose** if they accumulate:
- **2 bad tokens of each type** (Media, Tech, Force), or
- **4 bad tokens of any single type**

---

## Win Condition

The players **win** if the Anchor successfully resolves the final time slot without triggering the loss condition.
